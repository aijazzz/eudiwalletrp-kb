## What this site is

The EUDI Wallet RP Knowledge Base is an independent, plain-language guide for **relying parties**: organisations that want to accept the European Digital Identity Wallet (EUDI Wallet) to identify users or to receive verified attributes from them.

It covers registration with national registers, access and registration certificates, the data you may request, deadlines, technical essentials and the status of national registers by Member State.

## Who it is for

- product, compliance and legal teams assessing whether, when and how to accept the wallet;
- engineers integrating wallet presentations into a service;
- intermediaries offering verification services to relying parties.

Start with the [self-assessment](/check/) or [How to register](/registration-guide/registration-process/).

## Independence

This site is not affiliated with or endorsed by the European Commission, any Member State, any national registrar or supervisory body, or any wallet provider. It carries no advertising and no sponsored content.

## How content is sourced and reviewed

- **Primary sources first.** We write from the texts published in the Official Journal on [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/): the eIDAS Regulation as amended by Regulation (EU) 2024/1183, and its implementing regulations. Technical pages cite the Architecture and Reference Framework and the specifications it relies on, with their versions.
- **National information** comes only from official national sources, such as legislation, government sites and registrar announcements. Each entry shows its sources and the date it was last checked.
- **Citations.** Pages end with a "Sources" section. Article numbers, dates and deadlines are taken from the texts.
- **Clearly marked uncertainty.** Where something is not settled, could not be verified or is derived from the text, the page says so.
- **Review dates.** Each guide shows a "Last reviewed" date. Pages are reviewed when the underlying texts change.
