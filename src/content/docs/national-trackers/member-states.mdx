:::tip[Key points]
- Every Member State must set up **at least one national register** and designate **at least one registrar** for wallet-relying parties (Article 3(1) and (3) of [Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/848](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2025/848/oj/eng)), applying from **24 December 2026** (Article 11).
- Member States must tell the Commission which body keeps the register, and the Commission makes this public (Article 5a(18)(a) of Regulation (EU) No 910/2014, as amended). We have not yet found a published Commission list covering relying-party registrars.
- Below, **"Confirmed"** means the registrar is named in an adopted or published national act. Everything else is **not yet confirmed**, even where a bill names a body.
- We show contacts and links **only where an official source gives them**.
- This page uses the same data as the [/check/ self-assessment](/check/), so the two always match.
:::

## Member States not yet tracked

We have not yet researched Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czechia, Estonia, Greece, Hungary, Latvia, Lithuania, Malta, Romania, Slovakia and Slovenia. No conclusion should be drawn from their absence. Contact the national eID or digital ministry, or check the national official journal.

## How to read this page

- **Registrar:** the body that runs the national register of wallet-relying parties under Article 3(3) of 2025/848.
- **Check with:** the authority most likely to know, based on official sources. This is not a confirmation that it will be the registrar.
- **Proposed:** a body named in a bill or draft law that has not been adopted, as far as we know.
- Sources were checked on the date shown for each Member State. National laws are changing quickly; always confirm with the official source.

See [Methodology](/national-trackers/methodology/) for how we classify sources.
